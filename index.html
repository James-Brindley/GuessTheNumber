Guess The Dungeon

How To Play

Welcome to Guess The Dungeon — a mix of luck, risk, and clever choices!

🎯 Your Goal

Click squares on the grid to uncover attacks, gold, or nothing at all. Defeat the enemy before they defeat you!

  • 🟩 Green: Your attack — deal damage to the enemy.
  • 🟥 Red: Enemy attack — you take damage.
  • ⬛ Blank: Safe but resets your combo.
  • 🟨 Gold: Earn gold used to buy items in shops.

⚔️ Combat & Combos

  • Each successful hit raises your combo, increasing your damage up to ×2.
  • Taking damage or hitting a blank/gold tile resets your combo.
  • Both you and the enemy have separate combos — stay consistent to outpace them!

💰 Gold & Shops

  • Find gold squares during battles to earn coins.
  • Defeating Bosses rewards extra gold 💰.
  • Spend gold between rounds in the Shop to buy powerful items.
  • Items come in rarities: Common, Rare, Epic, and Legendary.

🧾 Items & Effects

Items give passive bonuses for your entire run — more attacks, healing, defense, gold gain, or special effects like Burn or Revive.

  • Hover the 🎒 button to view your collected items.
  • Some items add safe numbers — glowing squares that have a specified amout of safe squares.
  • Revive items automatically trigger when you die (once each).

🏆 Progression

  • Enemies grow stronger with each level.
  • Every 10th level is a Boss Fight with higher health and damage.
  • After a boss, enemies permanently scale up in strength.

🩸 Stats & Info

  • Hover over your hero or the enemy to see detailed stats like attack, health, regen, and gold bonuses.
  • Hover over the health bars for exact HP values.

⏸️ Controls

  • Press ⏸️ or hit ESC to pause the game.
  • Click Main Menu only when you’re sure — it resets your current run.

Good luck, adventurer — uncover the squares, master your luck, and conquer the dungeon!
